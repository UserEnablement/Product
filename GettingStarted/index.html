Module 01 · Getting Started

Learn the basics of logging in, choosing a plan, navigating the dashboard, and finding support.

This module walks new plan administrators through their first steps in Oliver Admin — from login to logout — while you build a Case File documenting what you find along the way.

Audience: New plan administrators, no prior experience Prerequisites: None — first module in the series
Opening

Before you begin

~2 min

Start with the practice activity, then complete the tasks in the live system. Use the guide as your reference whenever you need a reminder.

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

  • 1Log in to Oliver Admin using your credentials.
  • 2Select the correct plan if more than one plan is available.
  • 3Identify the main areas of the dashboard.
  • 4Open the Task List and Help menu.
  • 5Access Documentation and Send Feedback.
  • 6Change your password and review Account Settings.
  • 7Log out securely when finished.

Check

Quick Check

~2 min

Before you finish, make sure you can answer these questions.

  • Where do you enter your client code?
    On the login screen, before you enter your username and password.
  • How do you switch plans?
    From the plan selector shown after login, if more than one plan is available on your account.
  • Where do you find Documentation?
    Under the Help menu on the dashboard.
  • What should you do when you finish working?
    Log out securely so you're returned to the login screen.
